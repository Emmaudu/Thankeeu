# Taskeeu SEO/GEO Keyword Strategy — 20 Priority Clusters

Research date: 20 July 2026

This plan prioritises transactional search intent, relevance to Taskeeu's
actual service, and the strength of the current search-results gap. It does
not claim exact monthly Google Ads volume and does not guarantee a number-one
ranking. Rankings depend on indexing, links, brand authority, local signals,
content quality, competition, and ongoing performance.

## Supporting clusters

- City modifiers: Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, Kano, Benin City, Enugu,
  Warri, Owerri, Calabar, Uyo, Ilorin, Abeokuta, Onitsha and Jos.
- Service modifiers: pickup, delivery, shopping, market runs, document
  collection, queue assistance, welfare visits and property checks.
- Trust modifiers: verified errand runner, escrow-protected errand service,
  receipt proof, photo proof and task tracking.
- Comparison intent: Jiji alternative for errands and task marketplace vs
  classifieds, mapped to `/vs/jiji`.

## On-page and GEO implementation

- One canonical URL per intent cluster, with distinct titles, descriptions,
  headings, FAQs and service details.
- FAQ and service-oriented structured data generated from visible page copy.
- Direct answers written in plain language so search and AI answer engines can
  extract what the service does, where it operates, how payment works and what
  is excluded.
- Sitewide and contextual internal links connect service, city, diaspora,
  comparison and high-intent pages.
- All indexable landing pages are included in `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`,
  `llms.txt`, the React router and the prerender manifest.
- Medical and government-office pages explicitly avoid diagnosis,
  prescription decisions, impersonation, bribery and unlawful shortcuts.

## Post-deployment actions

1. Submit `https://taskeeu.com/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.
2. Request indexing for the five new high-intent URLs and the main hubs.
3. Connect Search Console and analytics data to track impressions, queries,
   click-through rate, conversions and crawl/indexing issues.
4. Earn relevant Nigerian business, diaspora-community and local-service
   citations; pages alone are not enough to outrank established domains.
5. Refresh titles and copy from real query data after 6–12 weeks, avoiding
   keyword stuffing or doorway-page duplication.
