## What Is an Errand Service Platform?

An errand service platform is a marketplace that connects people who need errands done with local, verified runners who can do them. Think of it as having a personal assistant on demand — except they are verified, rated by previous users, and you only pay once the errand is completed to your satisfaction.

In Nigeria, errand platforms typically handle:

- Grocery and market runs (Shoprite, Balogun, Oyingbo, Mile 12)
- Government office queuing (NIMC, FRSC, NIS passport offices, JAMB)
- Bill payments (EKEDC/NEPA, DSTV, school fees, rent)
- Pharmacy and hospital pickups
- Document collection and courier
- Personal shopping (clothing, electronics, gifts)
- Corporate office errands for SMEs and businesses

The market has grown significantly since 2020, driven by a generation of Lagos professionals who would rather pay a trusted runner ₦3,000 to spend four hours at NIMC than lose a full workday themselves.

## What to Look For in a Nigerian Errand Service Platform

When choosing a platform, these are the non-negotiables:

**1. Payment protection**  
Never pay for an errand in full before it is completed. The best platforms — like Taskeeu — hold your payment in escrow and release it only when you confirm the job is done. This protects you completely from fraud and no-shows.

**2. Runner verification**  
Your errand runner may be entering your neighbourhood, collecting your documents, or picking up your medication. They need to be identity-verified. Look for NIN or BVN verification as a minimum — not just a phone number.

**3. Transparent ratings and reviews**  
You should be able to see exactly who has handled this runner's previous errands, what they said, and how they rated the experience. Star ratings alone are not enough — you need full review text.

**4. Real-time communication**  
Errands rarely go perfectly according to plan. Your runner might need a substitution at the market, or the government office might have closed early. Real-time in-app chat and photo updates keep you in control throughout.

**5. Fair, competitive pricing**  
Fixed-price platforms set arbitrary rates that may not reflect the actual effort required. A marketplace like Taskeeu lets Taskers bid competitively, which means you pay a fair rate every time — and motivated runners deliver better work.

**6. Coverage where you actually need it**  
If you are in Gwarinpa, Ajah, Surulere, or Port Harcourt, you need a platform with active, verified runners in your specific area — not just in Lagos Island.
