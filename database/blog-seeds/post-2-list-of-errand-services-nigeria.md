## What Is an Errand Service?

An errand service is when you hire a trusted person — an errand runner or Tasker — to carry out a specific task or set of tasks on your behalf. This could be anything from picking up groceries to queuing at a government office to delivering a document across town.

In Nigeria, errand services have become a critical part of daily life for working professionals, business owners, elderly residents, and Nigerians in the diaspora who need on-the-ground support back home.

The right platform — like **Taskeeu** — connects you to identity-verified local runners who bid competitively for your task, so you always get a fair price and a reliable person.

### 2. Government Office Queuing

**What it is:** Hiring someone to physically attend a government office, hold your place in the queue, and complete your application, submission, or collection — so you do not waste a full day.

**Most requested offices in Nigeria:**
- NIMC (National Identity Management Commission) — NIN enrolment and corrections, Lagos Alausa, Abuja Garki, and across all states
- FRSC (Federal Road Safety Corps) — driver's licence, renewal, and corrections
- NIS (Nigerian Immigration Service) — passport application, renewal, collection at Ikoyi and Zone 3 Abuja
- JAMB — profile creation, result collection, correction of data
- CAC (Corporate Affairs Commission) — business registration, annual returns, name search
- LIRS / FIRS — tax payments and TCC collection
- Court Registry — filing of documents, collection of certified copies
- NAFDAC, SON, NCC, NBC, and other regulatory agency submissions

**On Taskeeu:** Describe the office, your specific requirement, and any documents your Tasker needs to carry or collect. A local Tasker attends in person and sends you real-time photo and video proof at every stage. This is one of the highest-demand errand categories on Taskeeu.
